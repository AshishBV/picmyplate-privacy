Delete your account and data

Last updated: 6 October 2026

You can delete your PicMyPlate account and everything stored for it at any time. There are two ways to do it: inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open PicMyPlate.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account & data and confirm.

This takes effect straight away. Your account is removed along with everything stored against it.

Option 2 — By email (no app needed)

Send an email to support@picmyplate.app from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my PicMyPlate account".

We use the sending address to confirm the request is really yours, so a request sent from a different address cannot be actioned. We will confirm once it is done, normally within 7 days and always within 30.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • All meals and their photos, weights and weight notes, goals, profile and custom foods stored on our server.
  • All progress photos stored in your private folder.
  • Your family membership, your family profile photo, the messages you sent, your shared daily numbers, reactions you left and reactions others left on your day.
  • Reports you filed and the people you blocked.
  • Your nutrition corrections.
  • Your AI usage counts and your crash reports.
  • Your subscription record: the plan, product, purchase token and end date our server kept to confirm a Google Play subscription.
  • A record of the family codes you tried, which is kept briefly to stop people guessing at other families' codes.

What is kept, and why

  • Pooled nutrition values. If you corrected a food's nutrition values, that correction contributed to a shared estimate for that dish. The shared estimate is recalculated from the individual corrections each time it is used, so once your corrections are deleted your contribution goes with them.
  • Reports about you. When the message is yours, we keep a copy of a message someone else reported for as long as we need it to act on the report — otherwise deleting an account would be a way to delete the evidence.
  • Family groups you created. A family group you created stays for its other members, with its shared meal plan; you are simply no longer in it.
  • The hashed device identifier. Our server keeps a one-way hash of your phone's app-specific device ID so the scan count cannot be reset by reinstalling. It is not stored against your account and cannot be turned back into the ID.
  • Backups. Routine backups may hold a copy for up to 30 days before being overwritten. They are not used for any other purpose.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, such as transaction records for a paid subscription. Payments are handled by Google Play, not by us.

Data on your phone is separate. Deleting your account removes what is on our server. Anything held locally on your device is removed by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings.

Subscriptions are cancelled separately. Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription and does not issue a refund. Cancel it in the Play Store under Subscriptions first, or it will keep renewing.

This cannot be undone

Deletion is permanent. There is no recovery period and no way for us to restore the data afterwards. If you want a copy first, email the address above and ask for an export before requesting deletion.
