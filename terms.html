1. What the app does

PicMyPlate estimates the nutrition of food you photograph, scan or search, tracks what you log, and calculates daily calorie and macronutrient targets.

2. Accuracy — please read

Nutrition values come from published reference tables where a food is known, and from an AI estimate where it is not. Estimates from a photograph are approximate. A photograph cannot show portion weight, how much oil or butter was used, or what is underneath what you can see, and two servings of the same dish can differ substantially.

Treat the numbers as a guide for spotting trends, not as measurements. Check the label wherever precision matters to you, and never rely on this app for decisions that require accurate nutrition information — for example insulin dosing, allergen avoidance, or a medically prescribed diet.

Calorie and macro targets are calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and standard activity multipliers. These are population averages and may not suit you.

3. Allergens and dietary restrictions

The app cannot reliably identify allergens or trace ingredients from a photograph, and dietary labels such as "vegetarian" or "vegan" are estimates based on the dish it thinks it sees. Never use PicMyPlate to decide whether a food is safe for an allergy or an intolerance.
