Privacy Policy — PicMyPlate

Last updated: 3 August 2026

PicMyPlate ("the app", "we", "us") is a nutrition and calorie-tracking app.

This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with,

and how you can delete it. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Contact: ashishbv11@gmail.com

Use this address for any privacy question or data-deletion request.

1. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account data (only if you create an account): your email address and an encrypted password (managed by our authentication provider — we never see your raw password).
  • Profile data: name, sex, age/date of birth, height, weight, activity level, dietary preference, and your goals.
  • Nutrition & activity logs: the foods you log, calories and macros, water intake, and weight entries.
  • Photos: food photos and progress photos you choose to capture or upload.
  • Health data (optional): if you connect Health Connect, we read four things — your steps, the distance you have walked, and your active and total calories burned — to work out how much exercise to add back to your daily calorie goal. Steps and distance are what the estimate is normally based on, because they are measured rather than estimated; the calorie figures are used when your phone provides them. We read nothing else from Health Connect, we never write to it, and we do not share this data. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health Connect app.
  • Family groups (optional): if you create or join a family group, your chosen display name and calorie target are visible to other members of that group.
  • Crash diagnostics: if the app crashes, we record what went wrong — the error message, the technical stack trace, your app version, and your phone's operating system version — and send it to our own servers so we can fix it. It is stripped of anything identifying before it is sent: email addresses, file paths, long numbers and access tokens are removed automatically. We do not use it for anything except fixing faults, and it is not shared.

We do not collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or track you

across other apps or websites.

2. How we use your data

  • To calculate your personalised calorie and macro goals.
  • To recognise food from photos and estimate nutrition (see AI processing below).
  • To store and sync your logs across your devices (only if you have an account).
  • To provide meal suggestions and progress tracking.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. AI processing of photos and text

When you scan a food photo, scan a label, describe a meal, or generate a meal

plan, the relevant image or text is sent — through our secure server — to

third-party AI providers for analysis and returned to you. These providers are:

  • Google (Gemini) and Groq / Cerebras (Llama models) — food photo, label, and text analysis.
  • USDA FoodData Central — nutrition data lookup for named foods.

Images and text are sent only to perform the analysis you requested. Please review

each provider's own privacy policy for how they handle transient request data.

4. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: all your logs are stored locally so the app works offline.
  • In the cloud (only if you create an account): your data is stored using Supabase (database, authentication, and private file storage). Progress and food photos are kept in a private storage bucket accessible only to you.

Access to your cloud data is protected so that one user cannot read another

user's data.

5. Data sharing

We share data only with the service providers strictly needed to run the app:

Supabase (hosting/storage/auth) and the AI providers listed in Section 3. We do

not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: go to Settings → Account → Delete account & data. This permanently removes your account and all associated data (logs, photos, profile, goals) from our servers and your device. This action is irreversible.
  • By email: if you cannot access the app, email ashishbv11@gmail.com from your account email address and request deletion. We will delete your data within 30 days.

Deleting the app from your device removes the local copy of your data; if you have

an account, use one of the methods above to also remove your cloud data.

7. Children

PicMyPlate is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in

your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS),

private storage, and per-user access controls. No method of transmission or

storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by

updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, contact ashishbv11@gmail.com.
